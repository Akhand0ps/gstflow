Compliance rules explained plainly. Statutory rules and schemas.

What makes an invoice strictly compliant with GST Rule 46?

An invoice must contain supplier legal name, address, GSTIN, consecutive serial number, date of issue, recipient name, address and GSTIN/UIN, HSN/SAC code, description, quantity, total value, taxable value, tax rate, and place of supply.

How does Section 170 whole-rupee rounding work?

Under Section 170 of the CGST Act, the amount of tax, interest or penalty is rounded off to the nearest rupee. Fractions of 50 paise or more are rounded up to one rupee; fractions under 50 paise are disregarded.

Why can CGST input credit never be used to pay SGST liability?

Under Section 49(5)(e) and (f), CGST credit is administered by the Central Government and SGST by State Governments. Cross-utilization between CGST and SGST is expressly prohibited by the Constitution.
